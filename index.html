About Us

We are an independent video game studio with a passion for creating original worlds, memorable characters, and stories that bring people together.

While video games are at the heart of what we do, our creativity doesn't stop there. We also develop animated films and other forms of entertainment, combining our experience in game development with our love for storytelling and cinema.

Gauchos vs Cowboys is our first animated film project, bringing together two iconic cultures in an action-packed story inspired by the history, landscapes, and spirit of the American and Argentine frontiers.

We believe great stories can live beyond a single medium — from games to films, and everything in between.
